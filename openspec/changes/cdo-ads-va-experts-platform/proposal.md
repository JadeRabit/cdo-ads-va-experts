## Why

CDO Ads & VA Experts needs a modern, high-converting web platform to showcase their digital agency services (Facebook Ads Management, Social Media Management, Virtual Assistant Services, and Digital Products) and convert visitors into leads through consultation bookings and digital product purchases. Currently lacking a professional online presence optimized for Vercel deployment with modern web technologies.

## What Changes

- **New Public Landing Page**: Hero section with dynamic headline, services showcase with interactive cards, funnel explainer widget, promo banner, testimonials carousel, and branded footer
- **New Client Booking & Lead Capture Portal**: Integrated consultation scheduler (Calendly/custom) and inquiry form capturing business details and ad budget
- **New Digital Products Store Front**: E-commerce module with grid layout for digital resources/templates with instant checkout/download
- **New Tech Stack Implementation**: Next.js App Router with TypeScript, Tailwind CSS + Shadcn UI + Lucide Icons, optimized for Vercel deployment
- **New Brand Styling**: Dark navy background (#0A0F1D), vibrant gold/yellow accents (#EAB308), crisp white typography

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `landing-page`: Public-facing marketing page with hero, services, funnel explainer, promos, testimonials, and footer
- `booking-portal`: Client consultation scheduling and lead capture forms with Calendly integration
- `digital-store`: E-commerce frontend for digital products with grid display and download/checkout flow
- `brand-theme`: Design system with dark navy/gold color palette, typography, and reusable UI components

### Modified Capabilities
- None (greenfield project)

## Impact

- **Codebase**: New Next.js project structure under `app/`, `components/`, `lib/` directories
- **Dependencies**: Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Shadcn UI, Lucide Icons, Calendly widget
- **Deployment**: Vercel-optimized build configuration, static generation where possible
- **Backend**: Supabase/Firebase integration for form submissions, bookings, and product delivery
- **Design System**: Reusable component library matching brand guidelines
